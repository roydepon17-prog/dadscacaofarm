## Brand & Style

The brand personality bridges lush, ancestral Visayan agricultural heritage with high-fashion artisanal confectionery. Rooted in the rich soil of Maayon, Capiz, the visual direction embodies artisanal single-estate cacao and holistic tree-to-bar luxury. The audience consists of discerning culinary connoisseurs, eco-conscious epicures, and luxury gift buyers who value traceability, direct-trade authenticity, and uncompromising craftsmanship.

The design movement blends **Haute Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactile Luxury**. It rejects clinical corporate fintech tropes in favor of an opulent, nocturnal tropical atmosphere: deep estate canopy greens layered with dark roasted nib tones, punctuated by warm alabaster surfaces and illuminated by refined metallic gold filigree lines. The interface feels like an archival estate tasting journal—poised, sensory, and deeply grounded in botanical origin.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive dark environment that mirrors the shade canopy of cacao agroforestry and the velvet depth of high-percentage dark chocolate:

- **Primary (`#1E3A2B` - Estate Canopy Green):** Anchors core actions, branded cards, and status surfaces. It reflects the dense, sun-filtered foliage of the Capiz farm.
- **Secondary (`#2C1810` - Deep Roasted Cacao):** Serves as the deep chromatic foundation, applied across structural containers, rich media framing, and card backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Refined Gold):** Applied with deliberate restraint to interactive highlights, delicate borders, badges, tasting notes, and micro-accents.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Alabaster):** Used for primary typography, editorial pulls, and high-contrast foreground details, preventing the dark mode palette from becoming muddy or unreadable.
- **Background & Canvas:** Deep chocolate-ebony (`#120B07` to `#1A100B`) forms the lowest canvas plane, allowing the `#1E3A2B` and `#2C1810` layers to emerge with dimensional weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a luxury tension between haute-couture heritage display forms and crisp contemporary geometry:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers intense drama and editorial stature. Used for estate provenance titles, chocolate bar editions, and tasting profiles. The extreme vertical stroke contrast echoes classic luxury publishing and vintage confectionery packaging.
- **Body & Supporting Copy (`Manrope`):** Delivers pristine structural readability against dark backgrounds. Its geometric yet slightly humanist architecture ensures complex agronomic notes, percentage breakdowns, and traceability metrics remain effortless to scan.
- **Label Caps:** Formatted with heavy letter spacing (`0.14em`) and uppercase styling to denote origin coordinates, cacao percentages (`70% DARK`), harvest seasons, and estate certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, asymmetric 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing gracefully to a 4-column flow on mobile viewports. Generous vertical breathing room replicates the quiet pace of estate terroir curation.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with dynamic column spans. Hero elements, packaging showcases, and terroir origin stories feature off-center alignments with `margin` of `3rem` and generous `space-2xl` section rhythms.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin` of `2rem`, standardizing tasting card decks into 2-column balancing grids.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with compact `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Tasting tags and ingredient specs shift into horizontal swipe rails.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered organic darkness, delicate hairline borders, and warm atmospheric illumination:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep, warm night-soil tone (`#120B07`). Flat, grounding base for ambient imagery.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Deep Estate Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`) or Cacao Liquor (`#1A100B`) containers flanked by a 1px hairline border in muted gold (`#D4AF37` at 22% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Active & Elevated Containers):** Floating interactive cards receive an ambient, warm gold drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Sensory Overlays):** Deep canopy tinted backdrop with a subtle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), encased in an articulated gold hairline frame.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a disciplined, tailored shape system (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor precise, architectural edges reminiscent of premium hand-folded chocolate wraps, crisp botanical labels, and gold foil packaging bands.

- Base interactive items (buttons, inputs, tasting chips) use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Cards, modal viewports, and estate image wraps employ `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges and harvest origin stamps utilize pill-shapes (`9999px`) to create purposeful visual counterpoints to the crisp architectural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`) with a refined gold border (`1px solid #D4AF37`) and alabaster text (`#FAF7F2`). Hover invokes a gentle gold glow and transitions background to a richer botanical shade.
- **Secondary / Terroir Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, gold text. Hover deepens background to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` and sharpens border to 100% opacity.
- **Text Button:** Uppercase `label-caps` in `#D4AF37`, underlined by a delicate hairline rule with smooth horizontal scale transitions on hover.

### Cards
- **Chocolate Bar & Collection Cards:** Base background in `#1E3A2B` or deep roasted brown `#1E100A`. Features a `1px` inner golden perimeter line (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`). Product imagery sits against a softly illuminated circular gradient imitating dappled tropical canopy sunlight.
- **Tasting Profile Cards:** Compact dark slate surfaces bearing percentage typography (`70%`, `85%`), tasting descriptor tags, and estate batch numbers.

### Chips & Tags
- Terroir and tasting notes (e.g., *Floral*, *Roasted Hazelnut*, *Sun-Dried*, *Maayon Single-Estate*) appear with a `0.25rem` radius, bordered in `rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.15)`, text in `#FAF7F2` (`body-sm`), accompanied by a micro gold dot indicator.

### Input Fields
- Dark roasted background (`#160D08`), bordered by `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Text renders in `#FAF7F2` with placeholder copy in muted warm sand (`#A69F94`). Focused states activate a crisp `#D4AF37` 1px outline with soft golden ambient focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox with `0.125rem` corner radius or circular radio, bordered in `#D4AF37`. Checked state fills with `#1E3A2B` accented with a centered gold check or pip.

### Estate Origin Badges (Custom Component)
- Distinctive botanical medallion containing vintage typography: batch number, cacao pod harvest date, and farmer signature lockup, framed with a double-layered gold micro-border.
